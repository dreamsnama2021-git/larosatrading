This Privacy Policy explains how La Rosa Rose General Trading Co. LLC collects, uses, stores and protects personal data when you visit larosatrading.com, contact us or do business with us, in line with the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021).

01Who we are

La Rosa Rose General Trading Co. LLC (“La Rosa”, “we”, “us” or “our”) is a general trading company based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, supplying interior solutions, procurement services, FMCG, IT supplies, logistics support and industrial spare parts across the GCC, India and Sri Lanka.

For the purposes of UAE data protection law, La Rosa is the controller of the personal data described in this policy. Our registered office is at Office M25, Warba Centre, Deira, P.O. Box 114388, Dubai, United Arab Emirates.

02Scope of this policy

This policy applies to personal data we process through:

  • our website, larosatrading.com, including the contact and quote request forms;
  • emails, phone calls, WhatsApp and other communications with us;
  • quotations, purchase orders, deliveries, invoices and after-sales support for our customers;
  • our dealings with suppliers, vendors and business partners.

It does not apply to third-party websites or services that we link to, which have their own privacy policies.

03Personal data we collect

We only collect the personal data we need. Depending on how you interact with us, this may include:

  • Identity and contact details: name, job title, company name, email address, phone number and business address.
  • Enquiry details: the content of messages, quote requests, project requirements and specifications you send us.
  • Transaction details: purchase orders, delivery addresses, invoices, payment records and trade licence or TRN details of business customers.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited and the date and time of your visit, collected automatically when you use our website.
  • Supplier data: contact details of supplier and vendor representatives and information needed for vendor registration.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data (such as health, biometric or religious data) through our website. Please do not include such data in messages you send us.

04How we use your personal data

We use personal data to:

  • respond to your enquiries and prepare quotations;
  • process, deliver and invoice orders, and provide installation and after-sales support;
  • manage our relationships with customers, suppliers and partners;
  • send service updates and, where you have agreed, information about our products and services;
  • operate, secure and improve our website;
  • meet our legal, tax (including UAE VAT), accounting and regulatory obligations;
  • establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

05Legal basis for processing

Under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law we process personal data on one or more of the following grounds:

  • Contract: where processing is necessary to provide a quotation or perform a contract with you or your organisation;
  • Legal obligation: where we must keep records or share information under UAE law;
  • Consent: for example, when you submit our contact form or subscribe to marketing. You may withdraw consent at any time;
  • Legitimate business interests: such as running and securing our business and website, where these are not overridden by your rights.

06Sharing your personal data

We do not sell or rent personal data. We share it only where needed, with:

  • manufacturers, suppliers and logistics partners who help us fulfil your order;
  • service providers who host our website, deliver our emails and form submissions, or support our IT systems, under confidentiality obligations;
  • professional advisers such as auditors, accountants and lawyers;
  • government, regulatory, customs and law-enforcement authorities in the UAE where required by law.

07International data transfers

Some of our service providers (for example, website hosting, email, form delivery, fonts and maps) may store or process data outside the UAE. Where personal data is transferred outside the UAE, we take steps to ensure it receives an adequate level of protection as required by the UAE Personal Data Protection Law, for example by using providers in countries with adequate data protection laws or under appropriate contractual safeguards.

08How long we keep your data

We keep personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy. Enquiries that do not lead to business are normally deleted within 24 months. Transaction, invoice and tax records are kept for the periods required by UAE law (generally at least five years, in line with UAE commercial and VAT record-keeping requirements). When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it securely.

09How we protect your data

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration. These include encrypted (HTTPS) connections to our website, restricted access to personal data on a need-to-know basis and secure storage of business records. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you and the relevant authority of a data breach where required by law.

10Your rights

Subject to the conditions of the UAE Personal Data Protection Law, you have the right to:

  • request access to the personal data we hold about you;
  • request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • request erasure of your data, where it is no longer needed or was processed unlawfully;
  • request restriction of processing, or object to processing, including for direct marketing;
  • receive your data in a structured, commonly used format and request its transfer;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting processing that took place before withdrawal.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at info@larosatrading.com. We may need to verify your identity before responding. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint with the UAE Data Office.

11Cookies and similar technologies

Our website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. To display the site we load fonts and icons from third-party providers (Google Fonts and cdnjs), and our contact page embeds a Google Map. These providers may receive your IP address and may set their own cookies under their own privacy policies. You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings; the website will continue to work.

12Children’s privacy

Our website and services are intended for businesses and are not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

13Third-party links

Our website may contain links to other websites, including social media pages. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites, and we encourage you to read their privacy policies.

14Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or in the law. The latest version will always be published on this page with its “last updated” date.
